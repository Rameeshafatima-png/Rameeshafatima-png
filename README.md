## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 📄 PaperMind AI · Research Paper Assistant (RAG)
[![Repo](https://img.shields.io/badge/View%20Repo-181717?style=flat-square&logo=github)](https://github.com/Rameeshafatima-png/PaperMind-AI)

Upload research paper PDFs and ask questions in plain language. PaperMind AI extracts text page by page, chunks it, creates embeddings, and stores them in a **FAISS** index. Answers come from **Google Gemini**, grounded only in retrieved context, and every answer includes the **source document, page number, and similarity score**.

`Python` `FastAPI` `Gemini` `FAISS` `PyMuPDF` `Pydantic`

---

### 🖼️ CIFAR-10 Image Classification · Deep Learning + Deployment
[![Repo](https://img.shields.io/badge/View%20Repo-181717?style=flat-square&logo=github)](https://github.com/Rameeshafatima-png/CIFAR10-_Image_Classification)

End-to-end image classification: preprocessing, **CNN** training and evaluation with TensorFlow/Keras, then deployment through **FastAPI**. Upload an image in a web interface and get the **predicted class and confidence score** across the 10 CIFAR-10 categories in real time.

`Python` `TensorFlow` `Keras` `FastAPI` `Matplotlib`

---

### ✉️ AI Email Generator (Dispatch) · Generative AI Web App
[![Repo](https://img.shields.io/badge/View%20Repo-181717?style=flat-square&logo=github)](https://github.com/Rameeshafatima-png/AI_Email_Generator)

Writes polished emails in seconds. Enter the recipient, purpose, and tone (**Professional, Friendly, or Formal**), and Google Gemini generates a complete email with an automatic subject line. Built with a **FastAPI** backend, **Pydantic** validation, a health-check endpoint, and proper error handling.

`Python` `FastAPI` `Gemini` `Pydantic` `JavaScript`
